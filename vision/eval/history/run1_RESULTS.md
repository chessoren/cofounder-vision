## Notes on honesty / limits of this benchmark

- The cursor in the synthetic videos is drawn with the same renderer the detector's template is built from, so cursor detection is tested on the ideal shape (+ codec + noise). The real demo-app recording uses its own recorder-drawn cursor.
- Perturbations are synthetic (resize, blur, JPEG, occluding rectangles); the Retina screenshot is a real 2x render, but templates are always cut from the same image they are searched in (before perturbation).
- Thresholds were set while writing the unit tests, before this benchmark was run; they were not tuned per condition.
